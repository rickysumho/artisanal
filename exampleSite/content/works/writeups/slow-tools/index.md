---
title: in praise of slow tools
date: 2026-05-12
type: journal
description: Why a little friction can protect good thinking.
tags: [tools, design]
---

Fast tools are wonderful until speed becomes the only measure of progress.

A slower tool can make space for judgment. It can ask for a title, a pause, or one deliberate choice before the work moves on.

The goal is not to make everything difficult. The goal is to make important actions feel intentional.
